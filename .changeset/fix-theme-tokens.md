In the paper (light) theme, cards and popovers no longer show a dark rim along their bottom and right edges. They now use the soft paper shadow they were designed with. Faint labels are easier to read in both themes, including on selected rows, active tabs and table heads: sort headers, captions, timestamps and the top bar's status line now meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1).
