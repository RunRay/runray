A `USD | tokens` switch beside %win picks the unit the headline figures lead with, and the choice persists. In token mode the dashboard, the rankings (ordered by tokens), the tool ranking, tokens by day, the Explorer list and the run header lead with token counts, with dollars beside them. Waste, savings and what-if stay in dollars, marked "at API prices", because most of those estimates are price differences with no token count behind them. %win now takes its percentages in the chosen unit, and its settings take a token budget.

In dollar mode, the dashboard's headline and Avg Session card now lead with dollars, and the Resource Breakdown follows the switch instead of its own Tokens / Spend toggle. The savings panel no longer shows token figures estimated from cost shares.
